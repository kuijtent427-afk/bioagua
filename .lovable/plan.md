

# BioAgua Chile — High-Conversion Lead Generation Website

## Overview
A 5-page Spanish-language website for BioAgua Chile, a water systems maintenance company targeting condominium administrators and industrial companies. Every page drives toward scheduling a free technical evaluation.

## Design System
- **Primary**: #0c202e (dark blue), **Secondary**: #1c3a5c, **Accent**: #2f6f8f
- **Light bg**: #f5f8fb, **CTA buttons**: #f4a261 (warm orange)
- Clean, technical, modern style with soft wave dividers (water theme)
- Rounded cards, large bold headlines, strong CTA contrast
- Mobile-first responsive, max-width centered layout
- Alternating white/light blue section backgrounds

## Pages

### 1. Home (`/`)
- **Hero**: Bold headline "Reduce fallas, costos y consumo en tus sistemas de agua" with two CTAs (evaluation + quote)
- **Problems section**: 4 icon cards (fallas, costos, sarro, emergencias)
- **Solutions section**: 3 cards (mantención, ionizadores, optimización)
- **Proof/stats bar**: 40% ahorro, <2hr respuesta, +50 clientes
- **CTA banner**: "Agenda evaluación técnica gratuita sin compromiso"

### 2. Nosotros (`/nosotros`)
- Headline about trust (+50 clients)
- Company values: expert team, prevention focus, 24/7 support, multi-sector experience
- CTA to contact

### 3. Empresas (`/empresas`)
- Hero targeting industrial cost reduction (40%)
- Problems section (industrial failures, inefficiency, scale, costs)
- Solutions with ionization as differentiator
- CTA: "Solicita auditoría técnica gratuita"

### 4. Condominios (`/condominios`)
- Hero addressing complaints and emergencies
- Problems (neighbor complaints, outages, night emergencies, repeated costs)
- Solutions (preventive maintenance, scale reduction, 24/7 support)
- Proof stats + CTA

### 5. Contacto (`/contacto`)
- Headline: "Agenda una evaluación técnica gratuita"
- Structured form: client type, system type, size, main problem, name, email, phone
- WhatsApp button visible, trust badges ("Respuesta en menos de 24h", "Sin compromiso")

## Shared Components
- **Navbar**: Logo + nav links + orange CTA button, mobile hamburger menu
- **Footer**: Contact info, WhatsApp link, quick links, copyright
- **Wave dividers**: SVG wave shapes between sections
- **CTA Banner**: Reusable component appearing every 2-3 sections

## Content
- All text in Spanish, concise and results-driven
- No generic marketing fluff — focused on savings, prevention, and results
- Every section answers "why act now"
